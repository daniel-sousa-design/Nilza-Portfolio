Nilza Lello Graphic Designer

About

With a background shaped by international and multidisciplinary experiences — from her formative training in Porto and the UK, to collaborative work in publishing and studio environments — Nilza approaches design as both a craft and a method of exploration.

Currently based in Porto, Nilza works at This is Pacifica, where she has the privilege of working on projects for clients like WOO, Solverde and Crédito Agrícola.
